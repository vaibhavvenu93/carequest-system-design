# Learner Progression System

## Purpose

The learner progression system turns certification training into a visible growth journey.

The goal is to make learners feel progress, identity, and momentum.

---

## Core Principle

People do not stay motivated by content alone.

They stay motivated by visible progress.

---

## Progression Layers

CareQuest can use:

- XP
- levels
- badges
- mission unlocks
- streaks
- readiness scores
- competency graphs
- role pathways

---

## Example Levels

| Level | Title | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Care Explorer | Learner has started foundational care training |
| 2 | Behavior Basics Learner | Learner understands core behavioral concepts |
| 3 | Reinforcement Apprentice | Learner can identify basic reinforcement strategies |
| 4 | Simulation Practitioner | Learner is practicing scenario-based responses |
| 5 | Certification Ready Learner | Learner is nearing ABAT/RBT readiness |

---

## Mission Unlock Logic

Learners unlock new missions based on:

- XP earned
- scenarios completed
- competency scores
- weak areas
- retry performance
- readiness thresholds

---

## Why This Matters

Traditional certification systems hide progress.

CareQuest makes progress visible.

Visible progress can increase motivation, completion, and learner confidence.
