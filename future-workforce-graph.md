# Future Workforce Graph

## Purpose

The workforce graph connects learning activity, simulation performance, competency signals, certification progress, and job readiness.

---

## Core Thesis

The future care workforce should not be represented only by resumes and certificates.

It should be represented by verified competency signals.

---

## Workforce Profile Data

A CareQuest workforce profile may include:

- learner role path
- certification goal
- completed missions
- simulation history
- competency strengths
- growth areas
- readiness score
- badges
- supervisor notes
- placement readiness
