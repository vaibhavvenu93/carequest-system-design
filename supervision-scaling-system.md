# Supervision Scaling System

## Purpose

The supervision scaling system explores how AI can help increase the capacity of human supervisors in neurodivergence care training.

---

## Core Problem

Care workforce training often depends on experienced supervisors.

But supervisors are limited.

This creates bottlenecks in:

- feedback
- fieldwork readiness
- competency validation
- learner confidence
- certification progression

---

## Core Principle

AI should not replace human supervisors.

AI should increase the number of learners a human supervisor can support effectively.

---

## Supervision Workflow

1. Learner completes simulation
2. AI supervisor reviews response
3. Competency gaps are identified
4. Coaching feedback is generated
5. Summary is created for human supervisor
6. Human supervisor reviews flagged cases
7. Learner receives targeted next missions

---

## What AI Can Handle

- first-pass response review
- pattern detection
- coaching summaries
- weak-area identification
- progress tracking
- recommended remediation

---

## What Humans Should Handle

- high-risk cases
- ethical judgment
- live clinical supervision
- certification sign-off
- complex emotional situations
- professional accountability

---

## Human Review Triggers

A case should be escalated to a human supervisor when:

- learner response suggests unsafe action
- scenario involves aggression or self-injury
- learner repeatedly fails same competency
- AI confidence is low
- certification decision is involved

---

## Long-Term Vision

The supervision system becomes an AI-assisted operating layer for care workforce scaling.

The goal is not to remove humans.

The goal is to help scarce experts support more learners safely.
